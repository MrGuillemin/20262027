Exercise 1 — Conjugate BE

Complete with the correct form of BE. Some sentences are negative.

Écris les mots manquants ou la phrase complète demandée.

  1. My friends ___ ready for our American road trip.
  2. I ___ excited about visiting California.
  3. Sarah ___ interested in Las Vegas. She prefers national parks.
  4. You ___ going to love the Grand Canyon!
  5. My brother and I ___ in New York yet. We are still in Washington D.C.
  6. Jack ___ very happy about our trip to San Francisco.
  7. Our next stop ___ going to be Niagara Falls.
  8. My parents ___ tired, so they want to continue the journey.
  9. We ___ ready to leave for California.
  10. The Grand Canyon ___ in New York.

Exercise 2 — BE going to

Complete with BE GOING TO and the verb in brackets. Some sentences are negative.

Écris les mots manquants ou la phrase complète demandée.

  1. Holly ___ the Golden Gate Bridge. (see)
  2. My friends ___ by plane. They prefer the car. (not / travel)
  3. I ___ lots of pictures at Niagara Falls. (take)
  4. Tom ___ Las Vegas because he doesn’t like big cities. (not / visit)
  5. You and your sister ___ two days in Los Angeles. (spend)
  6. Sarah ___ in Miami Beach. (swim)
  7. We ___ home before Sunday. (not / come)
  8. My cousin and I ___ Yosemite National Park. (explore)
  9. Jack ___ across the USA by car. (travel)
  10. I ___ a postcard in San Francisco. (buy)

Exercise 3 — Wishes, plans and purposes

Translate into English. Several common formulations are accepted.

Traduis en anglais. Une réponse non reconnue peut être une formulation correcte qui n’est pas encore prévue.

  1. J’aimerais visiter New York pour voir la Statue de la Liberté.
  2. Nous allons aller à San Francisco pour traverser le Golden Gate Bridge.
  3. Ma sœur adorerait visiter Yosemite pour voir les séquoias.
  4. Mes amis veulent aller à Los Angeles pour voir un match des Lakers.
  5. Tom ne va pas visiter Las Vegas. Il veut découvrir le Grand Canyon.
  6. Nous aimerions aller à Niagara Falls pour voir les immenses chutes d’eau.
  7. Je vais passer deux jours à Washington D.C. pour visiter la Maison-Blanche.
  8. Sarah ne veut pas aller à Miami. Elle aimerait visiter la Californie.
  9. J’aimerais aller à San Francisco pour voir le Golden Gate Bridge.
  10. Nous voulons visiter New York pour découvrir Central Park.

Exercise 4 — Rebuild the road trip

Put the stages in order and choose a chronological connector. Use First at the beginning, Then / Next / After that in the middle, and Finally at the end.

Indique l’ordre des étapes et choisis un connecteur chronologique adapté.

  1. We are going to spend our last afternoon on Miami Beach before going to the airport.
  2. On Wednesday morning, we are going to leave New York and drive to Washington D.C.
  3. We are going to arrive in New York on Monday morning and pick up our car at the airport.
  4. After Washington D.C., we are going to continue south and spend two days in Orlando.
  5. At 8 p.m., we are going to take our plane back home from Miami.
  6. We are going to spend our first two days in New York to visit the Statue of Liberty and Central Park.
  7. After Orlando, we are going to drive to Miami for the final part of our trip.
  8. In Washington D.C., we are going to spend one day exploring the city before leaving the next morning.
