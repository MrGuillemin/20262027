Exercise 1 — Connectors

Choose the correct answer.

Choisis la réponse qui complète correctement la phrase.

  1. Melbourne has historic buildings _____ modern architecture.and · also · so
  2. There are many green spaces, _____ people can relax and enjoy nature.and · also · so
  3. Melbourne is famous for its festivals. It is _____ famous for its sporting events.and · also · so
  4. You can travel by train _____ bus.and · also · so
  5. There are museums, _____ you can discover Aboriginal culture.and · also · so
  6. You can visit art galleries. You can _____ discover colourful street art.and · also · so
  7. Melbourne has many parks _____ gardens.and · also · so
  8. Trams are easy to use, _____ people can travel around the city easily.and · also · so
  9. Melbourne has beaches _____ large parks.and · also · so
  10. The city has many cycle paths, _____ people can travel by bike.and · also · so

Exercise 2 — Translate into English

Translate into English. Several common formulations are accepted.

Traduis en anglais. Une réponse non reconnue peut être une formulation correcte qui n’est pas encore prévue.

  1. Il y a beaucoup de choses à voir et à faire.
  2. Melbourne est une ville agréable parce qu’il y a beaucoup de parcs.
  3. Il y a quelques espaces verts dans le centre-ville.
  4. Melbourne est une ville animée parce qu’elle organise beaucoup de festivals.
  5. Il y a plus de 500 trams à Melbourne.
  6. Les habitants peuvent se détendre parce qu’il y a beaucoup de jardins.
  7. Il y a beaucoup d’événements sportifs dans la ville.
  8. J’aime Melbourne parce qu’il y a beaucoup de choses à faire.
  9. Il y a quelques musées dans le centre-ville.
  10. Il y a beaucoup de parcs et de jardins.

Exercise 3 — Reformulate with THERE IS / THERE ARE

Replace YOU HAVE with THERE IS or THERE ARE. Keep the quantity and all the information.

Reformule avec there is ou there are, en conservant toutes les informations.

  1. In Melbourne, you have many parks and gardens.
  2. In a great city, you have a lot of activities.
  3. In Melbourne, you have some street art.
  4. In a great city, you have good public transport.
  5. In Melbourne, you have more than 500 trams.
  6. In a great city, you have many shops and restaurants.
  7. In Melbourne, you have a lot of greenery.
  8. In a great city, you have an amusement park.
  9. In Melbourne, you have many museums.
  10. In a great city, you have some green spaces.
