Exercise 1 — BE or HAVE?

Complete with the correct form of BE or HAVE.

Écris les mots manquants ou la phrase complète demandée.

  1. Jack ___ a brother.
  2. I ___ 12 years old.
  3. My friends ___ blue eyes.
  4. Charlie ___ 15 years old.
  5. You ___ cold.
  6. My sister ___ long brown hair.
  7. We ___ hungry.
  8. He ___ a dog.
  9. They ___ very tall.
  10. My best friend ___ two sisters.

Challenge

Rewrite in the negative form: My sister has long brown hair. / He has a dog.

Exercise 2 — The simple present

Complete with the correct verb form. Some sentences are negative.

Écris les mots manquants ou la phrase complète demandée.

  1. I ___ in Nevers. (live)
  2. Jack ___ in Manchester. (live)
  3. My friends ___ football. (play)
  4. My best friend ___ football. (play)
  5. Jack and Charlie ___ in England. (live)
  6. My brother ___ tennis. (not play)
  7. The students ___ in London. (not live)
  8. My sister ___ football. (like)
  9. Mélina and Jade ___ basketball. (not like)
  10. The new student ___ in France. (not live)

Challenge

Rewrite with My best friend: My friends play football. / The students don't live in London.

Exercise 3 — Reformulation

Rewrite with the new subject. Make all the necessary changes.

Écris les mots manquants ou la phrase complète demandée.

  1. I am 12 years old. → He
  2. Jack lives in Manchester. → They
  3. I play football. → She
  4. She is hungry. → We
  5. They don't live in London. → He
  6. He doesn't play tennis. → I
  7. You are cold. → She
  8. I live in Nevers. → Jack
  9. We are 12 years old. → She
  10. They play basketball. → He

Challenge

Make this sentence negative: Jack lives in Nevers.

Exercise 4 — Meet Emma!

Use Emma's ID card to write at least five sentences about her. Then imagine you are Emma and rewrite with I.

Rédige des phrases complètes en respectant la consigne.

  1. Your text about Emma
  2. Your text as Emma
