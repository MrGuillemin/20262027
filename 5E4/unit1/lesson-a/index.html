Lesson A

Physical description — BE, HAVE and adjectives

10 questions are selected randomly each time. Check your answers, then try again for a new selection.

Exercise 1 — Reformulation

Rewrite each sentence with the new subject. Make all the necessary changes.

⭐ Challenge

Rewrite with HAVE GOT: He doesn't have glasses.

Exercise 2 — BE or HAVE?

Complete with the correct form of BE or HAVE.

⭐ Challenge

Write one sentence with BE and one sentence with HAVE.



Exercise 3 — Where is the adjective?

Put the words in the correct order to make a correct sentence.

⭐ Challenge

Describe yourself using two adjectives before a noun.
