Exercise 1 — Footy habits

Write complete sentences. Use the frequency words in brackets.

Écris des phrases complètes en utilisant les expressions de fréquence entre parenthèses.

  1. Jack + TV + friends (usually)
  2. Olivia + team scarf + school (every Friday)
  3. Noah + footy + friends (twice a week)
  4. Sophie + stadium + family (sometimes)
  5. Emma + Collingwood scarf + match days (often)
  6. Jack + Sydney Swans shirt + school (never)
  7. Olivia + footy + family (once a week)
  8. Noah + Sydney Swans matches + TV (every weekend)
  9. Emma + footy + friends (often)
  10. Jack + stadium + dad (every Saturday)

Exercise 2 — When something happens…

Write sentences with WHEN.

Construis deux propositions complètes au présent simple, reliées par WHEN.

  1. team + victory → Jack + sing + dance
  2. Olivia + clap + shout ← team + goal
  3. favourite player + goal → Noah + jump + smile
  4. Sophie + hug + family ← team + victory
  5. Emma + cheer + wave scarf ← team + goal
  6. team + defeat → Jack + feel + sad
  7. Olivia + smile + celebrate ← favourite player + goal
  8. team + victory → Noah + sing + cheer
  9. team + goal → Sophie + jump + clap
  10. Emma + sing + dance ← team + victory

Exercise 3 — Show your emotions!

Rewrite the sentences to express stronger feelings. Write two different sentences.

Écris deux reformulations différentes. Deux réponses correctes valent un point ; une seule vaut un demi-point.

  1. Emma loves the Collingwood Magpies.
  2. Footy is exciting.
  3. Olivia enjoys match days.
  4. The atmosphere at the stadium is amazing.
  5. Noah loves playing footy with his friends.
  6. The supporters are happy.
  7. Sophie loves going to the stadium with her family.
  8. Australian Rules Football is special to Australians.
  9. Jack loves the Sydney Swans.
  10. The match is exciting.
