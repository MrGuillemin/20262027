Exercise 1 — BECAUSE or ALSO

Choose the correct answer.

Choisis la réponse qui complète correctement la phrase.

  1. Melbourne is a great city ___ it has museums and art galleries.because · also
  2. I like Melbourne ___ it is large and lively.because · also
  3. There are many parks. There are ___ historic buildings.because · also
  4. Tourists like Melbourne ___ there are many things to see and do.because · also
  5. You can travel by bus. You can ___ travel by train or tram.because · also
  6. You can visit museums. You can ___ discover colourful street art.because · also
  7. Melbourne is interesting ___ there is a lot of street art.because · also
  8. There are many sporting events and ___ a lot of music festivals.because · also
  9. I love Melbourne ___ it has many green spaces.because · also
  10. The city has many cafés. It ___ has excellent restaurants.because · also

Exercise 2 — Translate into English

Translate into English. Several common formulations are accepted.

Traduis en anglais. Une réponse non reconnue peut être une formulation correcte qui n’est pas encore prévue.

  1. Il y a beaucoup de parcs à Melbourne.
  2. Il y a quelques ruelles dans le centre-ville.
  3. Il y a plus de 500 trams à Melbourne.
  4. Melbourne organise beaucoup de festivals de musique.
  5. Melbourne est une ville agréable parce qu’elle a beaucoup de parcs.
  6. J’aime Melbourne parce qu’il y a beaucoup de choses à faire.
  7. Les touristes aiment Melbourne parce qu’ils peuvent visiter beaucoup de musées.
  8. Melbourne est une ville animée parce qu’il y a beaucoup d’événements sportifs.

Exercise 3 — Reformulate with THERE IS / THERE ARE

Replace YOU HAVE with THERE IS or THERE ARE.

Reformule avec there is ou there are, en conservant toutes les informations.

  1. In Melbourne, you have many gardens and parks.
  2. In Melbourne, you have a lot of street art.
  3. In Melbourne, you have more than 500 trams.
  4. In the city centre, you have some historic buildings.
  5. In Melbourne, you have a lot of modern architecture.
  6. In Melbourne, you have many sporting events.
  7. In the city, you have some laneways.
  8. In Melbourne, you have a lot of music festivals.
