Exercise 1 — Conjugate BE

Complete with the correct form of BE. Some sentences are negative.

Écris les mots manquants ou la phrase complète demandée.

  1. I ___ ready for the road trip.
  2. My best friend ___ excited about the trip.
  3. We ___ in New York yet. We are still in Los Angeles.
  4. Sarah and Tom ___ tired. They want to continue the journey.
  5. The Grand Canyon ___ in California.
  6. You ___ ready to leave.
  7. My parents ___ with us. I am travelling with my friends.
  8. Our next stop ___ Miami Beach.
  9. I ___ tired this morning. (negative)
  10. The hotels ___ very expensive here.

Exercise 2 — BE going to

Complete with BE GOING TO and the verb in brackets. Some sentences are negative.

Écris les mots manquants ou la phrase complète demandée.

  1. We ___ New York. (visit)
  2. I ___ pictures of the Golden Gate Bridge. (take)
  3. My sister ___ in Miami Beach. (swim)
  4. Tom and his friends ___ across the USA by car. (travel)
  5. Sarah ___ Las Vegas because she doesn’t like big cities. (not / visit)
  6. My parents ___ with us. (not / come)
  7. You ___ the Grand Canyon. (see)
  8. Our group ___ in Los Angeles for three days. (stay)
  9. I ___ a postcard in San Francisco. (buy)
  10. We ___ a hotel near the beach. (book)

Exercise 3 — Wishes, plans and purposes

Translate into English. Several common formulations are accepted.

Traduis en anglais. Une réponse non reconnue peut être une formulation correcte qui n’est pas encore prévue.

  1. Nous allons visiter New York.
  2. Je ne vais pas visiter Las Vegas.
  3. Nous allons à New York pour voir la Statue de la Liberté.
  4. Mes amis vont prendre la voiture pour aller à Los Angeles.
  5. À Los Angeles, nous allons voir les Lakers jouer au basket.
  6. Ma sœur ne va pas se baigner à Miami Beach.
  7. Nous allons à Washington DC pour visiter la Maison-Blanche.
  8. Tom et Sarah ne vont pas prendre l’avion. Ils vont voyager en voiture.
  9. Je vais prendre des photos du Golden Gate Bridge.
  10. Nous allons à Miami pour nous baigner.

Exercise 4 — Rebuild the road trip

Put the stages in order and choose a chronological connector. Use First at the beginning, Then / Next / After that in the middle, and Finally at the end.

Indique l’ordre des étapes et choisis un connecteur chronologique adapté.

  1. We are going to arrive in New York City at 10 a.m. and pick up our car at the airport.
  2. Our next destination is Washington DC. We are going to drive there from New York.
  3. We are going to spend two days in Washington DC to visit the White House.
  4. Before going back home, we are going to relax and swim in Miami Beach.
  5. We are going to take the plane back home from Miami at 8 p.m.
