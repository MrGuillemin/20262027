Exercise 1 — Put the verbs in the past

Complete with the past simple form of the verb in brackets.

Écris les mots manquants ou la phrase complète demandée.

  1. During our trip, we ___ three nights in California. (spend)
  2. Lucy ___ to see the Statue of Liberty. (want)
  3. My parents ___ a small restaurant near our hotel. (find)
  4. We ___ our journey early in the morning. (start)
  5. Jack ___ lots of pictures in Yosemite National Park. (take)
  6. I ___ the sunset from the beach. (see)
  7. My sister and I ___ around Chicago for two hours. (walk)
  8. Our family ___ from Miami to Orlando. (drive)
  9. They ___ to Florida last summer. (go)
  10. Sarah ___ volleyball on Miami Beach. (play)

Exercise 2 — Choose the correct form

Choose the correct answer.

Choisis la réponse qui complète correctement la phrase.

  1. Next summer, my parents ___ California.visited · visit · are going to visit
  2. Two days ago, we ___ a boat trip.take · took · are going to take
  3. Usually, we ___ our holidays in July.start · started · are going to start
  4. Last summer, we ___ to Florida.go · went · are going to go
  5. Tomorrow, Jack ___ the Grand Canyon.saw · sees · is going to see
  6. Every year, Lucy ___ with her family.travels · travelled · is going to travel
  7. Next weekend, I ___ across the Golden Gate Bridge.walked · walk · am going to walk
  8. Last night, my friends ___ a great restaurant.find · found · are going to find
  9. Yesterday, we ___ Yosemite.visit · visited · are going to visit
  10. Next year, Sarah ___ to New York.went · goes · is going to go

Exercise 6 — Translate into English

Translate into English. Several common formulations are accepted.

Traduis en anglais. Une réponse non reconnue peut être une formulation correcte qui n’est pas encore prévue.

  1. L’été dernier, nous sommes allés en Californie.
  2. Nous avons commencé notre voyage à San Francisco.
  3. Ma sœur voulait visiter le Grand Canyon.
  4. Nous avons pris beaucoup de photos à Yosemite.
  5. Ensuite, nous avons conduit jusqu’à Las Vegas.
  6. Nous n'avons pas vu le célèbre panneau de Las Vegas.
  7. Mes parents n'ont pas trouvé de restaurant près de l'hôtel.
  8. Finalement, nous étions fatigués mais très heureux.
  9. Hier, nous avons visité New York.
  10. Jack a pris beaucoup de photos.
